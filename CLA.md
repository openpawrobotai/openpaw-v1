# Orbie Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0 · In force from 8 October 2026**

## Why this agreement exists

The Orbie hardware designs are published under CERN-OHL-S-2.0, a reciprocal
licence. Anyone who modifies those designs must publish their modifications on
the same terms.

Ayva Labs is not bound by that obligation in respect of its own work, because a
copyright holder is not bound by the licence it grants to others. This is what
allows the Company to publish an open reference design while also manufacturing
a commercial product that is not published.

That position holds only while the Company holds sufficient rights in the whole
of the design. A single contribution accepted without a licence grant broad
enough to permit relicensing would end it, permanently and without notice. This
agreement preserves it.

Contributors keep ownership of their work. Nothing here assigns copyright.

## If You are contributing on behalf of a company

The agreement above is for individuals contributing their own work. Where
contributions are made by employees or contractors in the course of work for a
company, and that company holds the resulting intellectual property, the company
must sign a separate entity-level agreement granting the same rights, with an
authorised signatory and a schedule listing the individuals covered.

If this applies to you, open an issue or write to legal@ayvalabs.com before
submitting, and an entity agreement will be provided.
