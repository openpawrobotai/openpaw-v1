The **NC** term forbids commercial use and the **SA** term forces derivative
works onto the same licence. Both matter here, so until the relationship has
been assessed by someone qualified, this repository is published for reference
and credit only:

- It is **not** relicensed under Apache-2.0 or CERN-OHL-S, unlike V2 and V3.
- It is **not** cleared for commercial use.
- No part of it should be treated as a component of a commercial product.

**V2 and V3 are independent designs.** A two-wheeled companion with a tilting
head and a custom dot-matrix display shares no mechanical design with a
spherical ball-bot, and the CAD history in those repositories supports that.
They are licensed on their own terms.

We are keeping V1 public rather than quietly deleting it, because the project
began by replicating someone else's work and pretending otherwise would be
worse than the licensing question itself. Credit is owed and is given here.

If you are Max Imagination or s60sc and want anything on this changed,
please open an issue — it will be actioned.
